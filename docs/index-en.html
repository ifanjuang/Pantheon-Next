AI is already in the project

Using AI no longer simply means opening ChatGPT.

It is entering design, rendering, research, office, messaging, and professional software. It also arrives through other participants: colleagues, project owners, consultants, contractors, and suppliers.

A contribution can therefore reach the project after several transformations whose context is no longer immediately visible.

The question is no longer only, “Do I use AI?” It becomes: “How do we keep working methodically when AI intervenes at several points in the project?”

Why Pantheon?

Why not simply use ChatGPT or Claude?

For many tasks, use them. These assistants are highly effective for research, summarising, drafting, analysis, comparison, or thinking through a question.

Pantheon addresses a different need: bringing those capabilities into the practice’s durable work without making the project depend on a succession of conversations, files, and tools.

AI assistant

Respond within a given context.

A conversation can be highly useful without becoming the durable state of the project.

Pantheon

Keep the thread of the work independently of the tool.

Sources, methods, intentions, disagreements, and decisions remain connected to the project over time.

A conversation is not a project record. An answer is not a decision. Retrieved information is not necessarily true, current, or applicable.

You can change AI. Your professional intelligence should not have to start again from zero.

Pantheon

The practice’s professional intelligence already exists.

It grows through people, projects, methods, references, experience, and decisions. Some of it is found in documents; some in working habits and in the reasons behind past choices.

Pantheon does not try to turn that culture into an AI model. It enables the part that can be made explicit to be structured, connected, shared, and transmitted.

Continuity

A person changes. The project continues.

Information, choices, and open issues remain understandable over time.

Transmission

A project can transmit more than its latest file.

The reasons behind a choice, alternatives explored, and lessons learned can remain connected. Transmission does not mean replacement. Pantheon preserves the contribution and its context; it does not claim to reproduce the person, their judgement, intuition, or sensitivity.

Consistency

Different tools, a shared professional framework.

The goal is not for every colleague to use the same AI, but for the practice to preserve a common way to understand, verify, and decide.

Independence

Tools can change without taking the method with them.

A model, agent, or software product can be replaced without forcing the practice to rebuild what it has learned.

Preserving does not mean freezing. Errors, discoveries, new projects, and new practices must also be able to evolve the practice’s professional intelligence.

Design

Design is not only optimisation.

AI can analyse a brief, explore layouts, compare variants, simulate, calculate, research references, or propose forms. It broadens the field of possibilities.

But a project also relates place, uses, resources, budget, technique, existing fabric, territory, and people. These dimensions can pull in different directions: this is where architectural judgement operates.

Ecology

Understand a living context.

Climate, ground, resources, existing fabric, material, use, and time extend beyond a single performance figure.

Ethics

Question what is desirable to do.

A possible solution is not necessarily right for the project owner, users, territory, or those who will build it.

Aesthetics

Give the project a sensory quality.

Light, proportion, material, relationship to landscape, emotion, and architectural culture cannot be reduced to a score.

An intuition may appear before it can be fully explained. A calculation may challenge it. An AI-generated proposal may open an unexpected direction. These contributions can interact without being confused.

Not everything that matters in a project can necessarily be calculated. And not everything that can be calculated should necessarily decide the project.

Pantheon does not define what good architecture is. It preserves the context in which the architect can exercise attention, culture, intuition, and judgement when AI participates in design.

Keep the tools that work for you

Pantheon does not ask you to start again from zero.

ChatGPT, Claude, OpenWebUI, local models, knowledge bases, and professional software remain specialised and replaceable. Pantheon adds professional continuity around them.

01

ChatGPT or Claude

For research, analysis, drafting, dialogue, and occasional thinking.

02

Projects and knowledge

For organising durable context without confusing it with temporary instructions given to an AI.

03

OpenWebUI and advanced environments

For bringing together models, knowledge, functions, workflows, skills, and specialised tools.

04

Agents such as Hermes

For mobilising models and tools to execute a task. Hermes remains external, optional, and carries no professional authority of its own.

The Cockpit is the surface where the professional reviews the project and its work states. Pantheon carries the professional framework: context, provenance, statuses, rules, scopes, and decisions. Hermes executes when it is mobilised. Models provide capabilities. The professional sets direction, chooses what deserves attention, and decides what carries consequences.

When producing becomes easy, attention becomes precious. The more systems information passes through, the more explicit its provenance, context, transformation, and authority need to remain.

Responsibility

Pantheon provides the framework. The practice defines the applicable rules.

Pantheon is neither an AI model, a professional rulebook, nor a regulatory authority. It does not by itself guarantee project compliance. Applicable rules, references, methods, limits, and responsibilities remain defined and maintained by the relevant professionals.
