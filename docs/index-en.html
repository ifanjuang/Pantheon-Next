The new problem

Answers are multiplying. The thread of the work must remain clear.

A case or project can bring together several people, several tools, many documents, and sometimes contradictory information. The challenge is no longer only to obtain an answer, but to understand what it is worth and how it fits into the work already done.

The Pantheon response

Preserve the continuity of professional reasoning.

Pantheon is not another artificial intelligence system. It provides a shared framework so people, tools, models, and knowledge can contribute to the same work without erasing its context, limitations, or decisions.

Origin

Every piece of information retains its provenance.

Something retrieved does not automatically become true, applicable, or authorised.

Status

Every contribution retains its status.

Facts, hypotheses, interpretations, alerts, and decisions are not conflated.

Limits

Uncertainty remains visible.

Missing information, contradictions, and required checks are not smoothed over.

Decision

Consequential decisions remain attributable.

A technical capability or a proposal never implies authorisation.
