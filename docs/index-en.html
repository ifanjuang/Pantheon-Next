Architects · teams · practices

Every AI in the practice.

One shared frame of work.

One colleague uses ChatGPT, another Claude or Gemini. Design, rendering, office, and professional software increasingly include their own AI functions. Project partners may also bring material that has already been produced or transformed by these systems.

This diversity is not a problem in itself. The difficulty starts when these uses enter the same project record, the same working method, and the same professional responsibility.

Pantheon frames the project-record flow: what enters, what is sent to the AI, what comes out, and what remains. It replaces neither your tools nor your method: it makes visible what the AI drew on — models, skills, knowledge, connections — and separates what is merely remembered from what can be cited.

One practice · many uses

AI is no longer a tool sitting beside the work. It is spreading through the work itself.

It appears in conversations, software, research, documents, and images. It also enters indirectly when a client, consultant, contractor, or supplier sends material that has already been assisted by AI.

The question is therefore not whether everyone should use the same AI. It is how different uses can enter shared professional work without scattering the practice’s context, methods, and responsibilities.

Architecture · in one minute

An AI never answers on its own. Pantheon makes visible what it actually drew on.

Behind any answer there are always four things: a model, a way of working, some knowledge, and sometimes connected tools. Pantheon replaces none of them. It names them, situates them, and keeps track of the ones that were really used.

Then a single question matters: is what has just been produced only a recollection, or does it become a reference the practice can rely on?

AI · models

The language capability.

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, a local model, or the AI built into professional software. They write, compare, and reason — and they remain interchangeable. None of them is the practice’s memory.

Skills

Bounded ways of working.

Writing a site report, comparing two technical notes, preparing a consultation. A skill states what is allowed, with which exhibits and which level of approval. Available does not mean authorised.

Knowledge · web

What the answer rests on.

Project documents, practice knowledge, and the web when the question calls for it. Each element keeps its origin, date, and scope: found is not verified.

Connections

The tools the AI can reach.

Mail, storage, professional software, external services. Reading is one thing; writing, sending, or committing the practice is another, and it requires explicit authorisation.

Two memories

Remembering is not the same as being a reference.

This is the most useful distinction in Pantheon, and the easiest to check on a real project: one memory fills itself in and proposes; the other retains nothing without a human review, and it is the only one that can be cited.

Fluid memory · assistant / Hermès side

It remembers, it proposes.

The thread of the conversation, your working habits, summaries, drafting preferences. It makes the tool comfortable and rewrites itself continuously.

  • Fills itself in, with no procedure
  • Useful, fast, replaceable
  • Carries no authority: nothing it retains justifies a decision on its own
Register memory · evidence register · Pantheon side

It retains what may be cited.

Information does not enter it because it is useful or often repeated: it enters because it was proposed as a candidate and then retained by a human.

  • Source, date, and supporting exhibit
  • Declared scope: project, practice, domain
  • Certainty level, review and withdrawal possible

Between the two there is a single door: a human review. Nothing moves from fluid memory into the register through accumulation, repetition, or model confidence.

remembered ≠ authoritative found online ≠ verified connected ≠ authorised

In practice. “The assistant has picked up that you prefer short site reports”: fluid memory, no consequence. “High-level ventilation was approved by the client on 12 August, with the test report attached”: that is a register candidate — it becomes citable only after review, with its source and scope. The first makes the work more comfortable; the second commits the practice.

Pantheon

Give collective work with AI an intelligible form.

Pantheon does not try to make every colleague work in the same way. It organises the reference points that become shared when AI participates in the practice’s work.

Its grammar can be read through three simple questions: who needs to pay attention, in what context, and according to what method?

Roles

Organise attention.

Sources, context, responsibility, history, time, cost, clarity: not every situation needs the same lens.

Spaces

Organise context.

A project, practice knowledge, a tool, or a decision can be connected without being collapsed into one thing.

Rites

Organise method.

When a situation deserves more attention, an explicit review method can be proposed instead of an improvised answer.

The mythology makes responsibilities identifiable; it is not an army of agents. Athena focuses on sources and coherence; Themis on responsibility and limits; Hestia on context; Mnemosyne on history; Chronos on dates and versions; Ploutos on cost. The detailed expression of these roles is still being stabilised.

Visible context

Do not just give AI context. Be able to understand the context you give it.

An answer depends on the information actually being used. Pantheon therefore aims to make the active context legible: the project, documents, project knowledge, prior decisions, work issues, and, when relevant, the tools being mobilised.

The professional can see what is proposed, what is selected, where it comes from, what scope it belongs to, and which version is in use.

Prioritise

Not everything carries the same weight.

A recent source, a decision, an assumption, and general practice knowledge are not presented in the same way.

Separate

Not everything belongs in the same place.

Project material remains distinct from general practice knowledge and from an assistant’s free-form memory.

Visualise

Context becomes inspectable.

The professional can understand which information is actually accompanying the request.

Adjust

Context is not fixed.

A suggested item can be added, removed, or replaced before relying on it.

Not all information has the same lifespan. A conversation may remain temporary; an item may belong durably to one project subject; knowledge may become reusable across the practice. Pantheon is designed so that these transitions remain scoped, traceable, and revisable rather than silent.

Rites · explicit method

Some situations deserve more than a quick answer.

A contradiction between sources, an answer that feels too convincing, a hidden assumption, or a polluted context are recurring situations. A Rite gives a bounded review method for that kind of tension.

A Rite is not an automation. It structures method; it does not turn Pantheon into an autonomous workflow or decide in place of the professional.

Concordance

Compare sources.

When several documents do not yet support the same conclusion.

Self-critique

Put a proposal under pressure.

When an answer feels convincing enough that it is useful to reread it as if it came from a third party.

Divergence

Widen before converging.

When the problem deserves several directions before a choice narrows the field too early.

Refoundation

Restart from clean context.

When a session has accumulated too many assumptions, instructions, or subjects to remain reliable.

Professional intelligence

Transmit what the practice learns without standardising the people who work there.

The practice’s professional intelligence already exists. It develops through projects, experience, and decisions, carried by the people who contribute to them.

Pantheon does not try to turn that culture into an AI model. It structures the part that can be made explicit, connected, shared, discussed, and transmitted.

Continuity

A person changes. The project continues.

Information, choices, and open issues remain understandable over time.

Transmission

A project transmits more than its latest file.

The reasons behind choices and lessons learned can remain connected. Transmission does not mean replacement.

Consistency

Different practices, shared reference points.

The practice does not need to impose the same AI; it can preserve shared reference points for understanding, verifying, and deciding.

Independence

Tools remain replaceable.

A model, agent, or software product can change without taking everything the practice has learned with it.

Preserving does not mean freezing. Errors, discoveries, new projects, and new practices must also be able to evolve the framework.

Design

Design is not only optimisation.

AI can analyse a brief, explore layouts, compare variants, simulate, calculate, research references, or propose forms. It greatly broadens the field of possibilities.

But a project also relates place, uses, resources, budget, technique, existing fabric, territory, and people. These dimensions can pull in different directions: this is where architectural judgement operates.

Ecology

Understand a living context.

Climate, ground, resources, existing fabric, material, use, and time extend beyond a single performance figure.

Ethics

Question what is desirable to do.

A possible solution is not necessarily right for the client, users, territory, or those who will build it.

Aesthetics

Give the project a sensory quality.

Light, proportion, material, relationship to landscape, emotion, and architectural culture cannot be reduced to a score.

An intuition may appear before it can be fully explained. A calculation may challenge it. An AI-generated proposal may open an unexpected direction. These contributions can interact without being confused.

Not everything that matters in a project can necessarily be calculated. And not everything that can be calculated should necessarily decide the project.

Pantheon does not define what good architecture is. It preserves the context and distinctions needed for the architect to retain attention, culture, intuition, and judgement when AI participates in design.

Keep your tools

A shared framework does not require a single tool.

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, OpenWebUI, local models, and professional software can remain specialised and replaceable. Pantheon is not intended to become the router for all of them.

Pantheon carries the professional framework. Hermes executes when mobilised. Models provide capabilities. The Cockpit exposes useful information and work states. The professional retains direction and consequential decisions.

Responsibility

Pantheon provides the framework. The practice defines the applicable rules.

Pantheon is not an AI model, a professional regulation, or a regulatory authority. It cannot by itself guarantee project compliance. Applicable rules, references, methods, limits, and responsibilities remain defined and maintained by the relevant professionals.
