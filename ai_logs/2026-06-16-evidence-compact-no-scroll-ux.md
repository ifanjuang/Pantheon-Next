## Change

The evidence mobile mockup now uses a compact viewport pattern:

- no page scroll;
- fixed compact top bar;
- minimized `Infos` button for global guidance;
- one card visible at a time;
- horizontal swipe for subjects;
- vertical swipe for matters / affaires;
- visible action buttons on each card;
- first-use tutorial bubbles;
- card buttons for `Source`, `Description` and `Reco.`;
- detail panel split into sections separated by lines: Source, Description, Recommandation, Conséquences, Limite.
