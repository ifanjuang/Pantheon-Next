## Change

Added a dedicated Biche de Cerynie scene card for the Hercule AI literacy assets.

The card preserves the existing repository angle from `hercule_ai_discipline.md`:

- the Biche represents fine intention before the prompt;
- the discipline is to formulate without reducing;
- a fair request begins before the visible prompt.

The card refines the angle as:

- pre-prompt as technical and methodological preparation;
- the prompt as the last sentence of an invisible preparation;
- intention depending not only on wording, but also on model, system instructions, injected context, sources, memory, tools, skills, agentic mode, generation parameters and retrieval parameters;
- the risk that a correct answer can be produced in an environment that already displaced the intention.

Key retained formulas:

```text
Preparer le milieu de l'intention.
Capturer l'intention vivante.
Le prompt n'est que la derniere phrase d'une preparation invisible.
Une demande juste ne commence pas par le prompt. Elle commence par le choix du milieu qui pourra la recevoir.
Une intention vivante peut mourir dans un mauvais milieu.
```
