## Pantheon grammar

The page keeps the seven currently canonical Roles as separate viewpoints of judgment:

- ATHENA — structure;
- ARGOS — source quality, evidence and traceability;
- THEMIS — limits, risk and approval boundaries;
- APOLLO — quality, completeness and delivery readiness;
- HEPHAISTOS — fabrication / candidate composition;
- IRIS — transmission and formulation;
- ZEUS — arbitration of status and next procedure.

The prose was adjusted to avoid presenting them as workers. Concrete work remains on the executor side.

Task Contract remains the junction between a real mission and the useful viewpoints, scope, constraints, approvals, expected evidence, allowed/forbidden outputs and memory rules.
