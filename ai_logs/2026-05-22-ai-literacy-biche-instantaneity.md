## Change

Refined the Biche de Cerynie scene card around the stronger mythological angle:

```text
Apprivoiser l'instantaneite.
```

The previous framing `Connaitre la machine qui ecoute` was accurate but too technical and not close enough to the legend.

The updated framing keeps the system-predictability layer while making the surface language more mythological and pedagogical:

- the Biche is fast, precious and fragile;
- AI answers instantly;
- a fragile intention should not be thrown into speed;
- the system must be approached before being prompted;
- a good prompt is not only clear or elegant, it is adjusted to the machine that listens;
- the first prompt gives a trajectory, and later corrections may improve the surface without repairing a wrong direction.

Key retained formulas:

```text
L'IA repond dans l'instant. L'intention demande une approche.
Un bon prompt n'est pas seulement clair. Il est ajuste a la machine qui l'ecoute.
Une intention fragile ne se jette pas dans la vitesse.
L'IA corrige bien une forme. Elle repare moins bien une direction mal donnee.
```
