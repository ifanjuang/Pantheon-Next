## Content summary

The note frames Hercules as the discipline that follows Ulysses:

- Ulysses identifies traps in the human relationship to AI.
- Hercules builds active professional habits to hold AI forces without surrendering judgment.

The twelve labors are mapped to diversified AI-use registers:

1. apparent power;
2. proliferation of possibles;
3. fine intention;
4. dossier chaos;
5. intellectual matter;
6. attention ecology;
7. action threshold;
8. autonomy and finality;
9. legitimacy and mandate;
10. distributed organization;
11. deliverable maturation;
12. final threshold.
