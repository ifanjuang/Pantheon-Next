## Change

Refined the Sanglier d'Erymanthe scene card to clarify that slowing down the raw dossier is not passive delay.

The updated framing is:

```text
Ralentir pour mieux diriger et cadrer.
```

The card now makes explicit that:

- slowing down is a steering maneuver;
- raw dossier chaos must be made orientable before being synthesized;
- constituting context means choosing inputs, statuses, perimeter, source authority, exclusions and expected treatment;
- a clear AI synthesis can hide an unguided context;
- slowing the dossier is not slowing the work, but regaining direction.

Key retained formulas:

```text
Ralentir n'est pas attendre. C'est reprendre la direction.
Ralentir le dossier, ce n'est pas ralentir le travail. C'est reprendre la main sur sa direction.
Un dossier brut n'est pas encore un contexte.
La synthese ne remplace pas le cadrage.
```
