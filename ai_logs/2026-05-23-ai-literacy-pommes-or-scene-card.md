## Change

Added a dedicated Pommes d'Or scene card for the Hercule AI literacy assets.

The card reframes the golden apples around maturation under responsibility rather than simple polish or delayed delivery:

- the Pommes are distinct from Boeufs and Cerbere;
- the Boeufs compose the augmented work harness;
- the Pommes mature an output into an assumable deliverable;
- Cerbere will govern what remains, what passes, and what becomes memory or archive;
- AI can produce polished outputs beyond the user's real domain of expertise;
- AI versatility does not transfer expertise or responsibility;
- maturation includes reducing the deliverable to what is necessary, verifiable, situated, proportionate and assumable;
- the Atlas motif is used to show that AI may bring the fruit, but the human still carries the sky of responsibility.

Key retained formulas:

```text
Murir sous responsabilite.
Un livrable mur n'est pas ce que l'IA peut produire. C'est ce que nous pouvons porter sans trahir notre responsabilite.
Avant de cueillir le fruit, verifie le ciel que tu devras porter.
Murir un livrable, c'est decider ce qu'on renonce a garder et ce qu'on accepte de porter.
La polyvalence de l'IA ne nous rend pas experts de tous les domaines.
Un fruit trop lourd n'est pas un meilleur fruit. C'est une charge mal murie.
```
