## Why

The user questioned whether Pantheon risks duplicating OpenWebUI and Hermes when those tools are well configured with prompts, agents and skills.

The product answer is that Pantheon should not replace those tools.

Pantheon should provide the governed configuration and professional method layer that makes them usable in responsibility-bearing dossiers.

## Product doctrine added

Core thesis:

```text
OpenWebUI + Hermes gives power.
Pantheon gives professional control.
```

Market sentence:

```text
Pantheon is the governed configuration layer between AI tools and professional responsibility.
```

Main pillars:

```text
1. Governed configuration
2. Evidence-first workflow
3. Decision memory
```
