## Change

Added a dedicated Hydre scene card for the Hercule AI literacy assets.

The card preserves the existing repository angle from `hercule_ai_discipline.md`:

- the Hydre represents generative proliferation;
- the discipline is not to suppress multiplicity, but to explore and then close;
- possibility is not choice.

The card refines the angle as:

- deciding in a multitude of possible solutions;
- isolating one path for deep work even when other paths remain possible or potentially better;
- preventing the initial intention from diluting into a catalogue;
- distinguishing opening, classifying, isolating and deciding;
- naming the irreducible remainder that must be carried to human arbitration.

Key retained formulas:

```text
Trancher dans la multitude.
Isoler pour approfondir.
La possibilite n'est pas le choix.
Savoir decider, c'est savoir se concentrer sur une voie sans exiger qu'elle soit la seule possible.
L'IA ouvre tellement de portes qu'elle peut nous faire oublier qu'avancer exige d'en fermer.
```
