## Why

The previous strategy considered a combined diptych.

The chosen editorial direction is now to separate one board per journey:

```text
Board 1: Ulysse — Deceler les pieges
Board 2: Hercule — Maitriser les pouvoirs
Board 3: optional Ulysse / Hercule bridge
```

This avoids overloading a single image with too many scenes, too much text and artificial symmetry.
