## Change

Refined the Ceinture d'Hippolyte scene card to make the mythological and governance hierarchy sharper.

The main retained formula is now:

```text
Le don devient prise quand la transmission perd son mandat.
```

This was selected as the strongest formulation because it:

- directly mirrors the legend: Hippolyte could give the belt, but suspicion turns transmission into seizure;
- applies cleanly to AI systems: a datum can be given to a model without being given to every agent, tool, connector, MCP server, API or memory;
- differentiates the Ceinture from the Taureau and Juments by focusing on legitimate passage rather than action conditions or autonomy duration.

The card now also clarifies the hierarchy:

```text
Plaque: Legitimer la transmission
Maxime principale: Le don devient prise quand la transmission perd son mandat.
Maxime structurante: La transmission legitime n'est pas un acces. C'est un mandat situe.
Maxime operationnelle: Une donnee donnee au modele n'est pas donnee a tous les outils.
Maxime d'alerte: La discretion de la reponse ne garantit pas la legitimite de la transmission.
```

A new conditions section specifies the minimum elements of a legitimate transmission mandate:

- finality;
- recipient;
- necessity;
- perimeter;
- status;
- duration;
- trace;
- responsibility.
