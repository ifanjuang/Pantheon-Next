## Change

Added a dedicated Taureau de Crete scene card for the Hercule AI literacy assets.

The card reframes the Cretan Bull around action governance rather than prohibition:

- the Taureau marks the threshold where AI moves from output to action;
- the central discipline is directing action by establishing conditions;
- tool availability is not a general right of action;
- answer, prepare and act must remain distinct regimes;
- action requires direction, perimeter, right, limit, validation, trace and stopping point.

The card also uses deeper mythological layers:

- Minos receives a force with a destination, but keeps it;
- a force without destination becomes ravaging;
- Hercule does not destroy the bull, he captures it alive and makes it governable;
- false control is treated as a false sacrifice;
- poorly governed action can create operational Minotaurs: semi-valid, semi-action, semi-memory, semi-decision hybrids.

Key retained formulas:

```text
Diriger par les conditions d'action.
Gouverner l'action, c'est etablir les conditions dans lesquelles la force peut agir juste.
Un outil disponible n'est pas un droit general.
Une sortie candidate ne doit pas devenir action par glissement.
Repondre propose. Preparer oriente. Agir engage.
```
