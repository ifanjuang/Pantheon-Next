## Change

Added a theoretical preface for the Ulysse AI literacy corpus.

The preface frames the Ulysse journey as a cartography of human cognitive limits amplified by AI rather than as a simple list of technical AI risks.

Core ideas:

- AI does not create human biases from nothing;
- it accelerates, organizes and beautifies existing human distortions;
- AI objectivity is always situated by prompt, corpus, sources, memory, context and user expectations;
- each Ulysse stop reveals a distinct amplified motif;
- the phrase `L'IA ne fait pas seulement des erreurs. Elle extrapole les notres.` is retained as a central thesis.
