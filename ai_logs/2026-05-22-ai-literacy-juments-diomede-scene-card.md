## Change

Added a dedicated Juments de Diomede scene card for the Hercule AI literacy assets.

The card reframes the mares around autonomy, duration and stopping conditions:

- the Juments are distinct from the Taureau;
- an act is controlled by permission;
- autonomy is controlled by term;
- useful autonomy needs a clear result threshold coherent with the task;
- agents that continue too long can become an alibi for human indecision;
- workflows and agents must receive mission, perimeter, iteration budget, escalation thresholds, stopping criteria and human return point.

Key retained formulas:

```text
Donner un terme a l'autonomie.
Un acte se controle par permission. Une autonomie se controle par terme.
L'agent qui continue trop longtemps peut devenir l'alibi de notre indecision.
Continuer n'est pas toujours approfondir. Parfois, continuer evite de decider.
L'autonomie juste ne sait pas seulement poursuivre. Elle sait rendre la main.
```
